Glassline Motion Lab

An original, working recreation of the interaction patterns documented or reported for iOS 27: adjustable glass material, spring-driven app launch and dismissal, velocity-projected gestures, interactive back navigation, a collapsing toolbar, an assistant that lives in the top pill, and notification summaries. Every animation is instrumented, so you can export its curve and check it against a screen recording from a real device.
